OUR HISTORY

1944 → Today

Eight decades of sport, people and memories.

1944

The Beginning

Al Ahli Sporting Club was founded in Amman, with its first headquarters at the Circassian Charity Association in Ras Al-Ain.

1986

A New Home

The club's current headquarters in Bayader Wadi Al-Seer opened and became a major part of the club's modern identity.

TODAY

The Legacy Continues

Sport, culture, facilities and community continue to bring new generations through the doors of Al Ahli.
